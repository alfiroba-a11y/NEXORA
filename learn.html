HOW NEXORA WORKS

From market selection to outcome

  1. Choose a volatility marketSelect a market from the NEXORA market selector.
  2. Choose your predictionFor a digit contract, choose Even or Odd.
  3. Set your stake and controlsChoose a stake you can afford to lose. Adjust target profit, stop loss, and multiplier.
  4. Follow the active contractUse the running contract card to track the countdown or stop a Demo contract.
  5. Review the outcomeA contract settles as a win or loss at its stated outcome.

RISK REMINDER

Good decisions start with risk management.

  • Only trade amounts you can afford to lose.
  • A past result never guarantees a future outcome.
  • Use a fixed stake plan, target, and stop loss.
  • Take breaks. Do not chase losses.
